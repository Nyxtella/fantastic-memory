Project 03 Sep – Nov 2024 Term Project · NISER Advisor: Anamitra Mukherjee

Study of 2- and 3-Body Bound States

A term project on bound states of two and three particles in lattice Hamiltonians with next-nearest-neighbour interactions, studied with Green's functions.

Stage 01 · Context

Bound states in interaction-limited systems

Term project at NISER (September–November 2024), advised by Anamitra Mukherjee, on 2- and 3-body bound states in Next-Nearest-Neighbor (NNN) interaction limited Hamiltonian systems.

Stage 02 · Method

Green's function equation of motion

The bound states were studied using the Green's function equation of motion.

Stage 03 · Analysis

Stability and phase separation diagrams

The spectral function was used to determine bound state stability, and phase separation diagrams were created for such states.

Stage 04 · Extension

Beyond the continuum

The analysis included bound states above the continuum, and tried to find physical realizations of such systems.
