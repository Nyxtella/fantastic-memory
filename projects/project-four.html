Project 04 Selected Project · NISER Interferometry

Geometrical Phase using Mach–Zehnder Interferometry

A project that simulated and experimentally studied the geometrical phase using a Mach–Zehnder interferometric setup, later presented as a poster on geometric phase with Michelson interferometry.

Stage 01 · Context

The geometrical phase

A selected research project on the geometrical phase, carried out with interferometric techniques. Mach–Zehnder and Michelson interferometry, along with geometrical phase, are among the experimental techniques developed through this work.

Stage 02 · Simulation

Simulating the phase

The geometrical phase was simulated for a Mach–Zehnder interferometric arrangement.

Stage 03 · Experiment

Mach–Zehnder interferometer

The geometrical phase was studied experimentally using a Mach–Zehnder interferometric setup.

Stage 04 · Presentation

Poster at NISER Open Lab

Presented as a poster, “Study of Geometric Phase using Michelson Interferometry”, at the NISER Open Lab (2025).
